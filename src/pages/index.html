The curriculum

The 7 Threshold Keys

This phase does not instantly cure the chaos; it builds the armor required to survive it. We are forging a completely new language between you and your dog. Before we face the storm of their triggers, we install these core mechanics in a quiet, controlled space.

  1. The Gateway

    The door routine

    The mastery of transitions. Your dog learns to enter the world in a state of calm, and that a regulated nervous system is the key to everything on the other side.

  2. The Anchor

    Handler focus

    The invisible tether. Your dog learns to tune out environmental static and lock onto you, so that when overwhelm hits they have a safe target.

  3. The Sanctuary

    Place

    The psychological off-switch. A physical boundary where your dog can down-regulate, and where you get a required moment of peace.

  4. The Yield

    Sit

    The foundational pause. A replacement behavior that halts forward momentum, breaks fixation, and buys a single grounded breath.

  5. The Root

    Down

    Physical grounding. The directive to settle the nervous system completely, even in the center of unrest and overstimulation.

  6. The Stillness

    Stay

    The forge of canine confidence. Holding their ground gives the brain a concrete job and proves they can handle the chaos around them.

  7. The Return

    Recall

    The literal lifeline. A slipped leash, an open door, a sprint toward the street: this is the difference between tragedy and safety.
