Meet your alchemist

Stevie Rossi

Trainer & Behavior Specialist

You are exhausted. And I know exactly how heavy it feels to hold the leash of a dog you cannot fully predict.

I'm the founder of Threshold K9 and a Training Specialist at Longmont Humane Society. I built this forge for the dogs living at the edge of overwhelm: the reactive dogs, the intense dogs, the complicated dogs, and the dogs other trainers may not know how to reach.

A lifetime among dogs

My relationship with dogs started early. I grew up in a home where there were rarely fewer than 8 to 12 dogs at a time, with puppies moving through every stage of development. I learned by watching, handling, working, and living alongside dogs long before I ever called it "training."

As I grew older, that love became a fascination with how dogs think, learn, communicate, and survive stress. I studied training techniques, built my own handling systems, and began working with the dogs most people were afraid to touch: reactive dogs with bite histories, dogs who could not exist calmly around other dogs, and extreme resource guarding cases that needed more than basic obedience.

When I began working in shelter behavior, that passion exploded.

I saw the gap clearly: the dogs who need skilled training the most are often the ones denied it because of liability, fear, or lack of experience. Threshold K9 exists to be their lifeline.

Why service dogs

A bridge back into the world

My passion for service dog work comes from somewhere even deeper. I grew up in a household shaped by disability. I live with it myself, and I watched people I love move through the world without the support, access, and partnership they deserved.

When I began training my first service dog, everything in my life changed. I understood, in my body, what it means for a dog to become more than a companion. A service dog can mean independence. Safety. Grounding. Access.

That is why I created the Ascension Track: to help shape carefully selected dogs into stable, skilled, task-trained partners who can support their handlers with clarity, confidence, and real-world reliability.

The approach

Regulation first

My work is rooted in hard behavioral science and forged in the real-world trenches of reactivity, aggression, bite histories, impulse control, resource guarding, service dog foundations, public access skills, and nervous system overload.

Before we ask a dog to "behave," we teach them how to feel safe, think clearly, and move through the world with confidence.

Behavioral Alchemy

Rewiring overwhelmed nervous systems, managing reactivity and aggression, and building clear, reliable safety boundaries.

Service Dog Mastery

Shaping raw potential into focused, task-trained, public-access partners for handlers who need more than obedience.

Clinical Empathy

Translating your dog's chaotic stress signals into a tactical, real-world communication system you can actually use.

Training with purpose. Partnership. And a little bit of magic.

Threshold K9 is for the dogs standing at the edge of overwhelm, and the humans ready to walk through that threshold with them.
