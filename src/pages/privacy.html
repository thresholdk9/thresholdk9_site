What I collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address, your dog's name, breed and age, and your message.
  • Intake form and waiver (new clients): your contact details, city and zip code, an emergency contact, your dog's health, veterinary and behavior history, your rabies certificate, your training goals, and your typed signature with the date and time you signed.
  • Booking and payments: handled by Square. Square collects your booking details and payment information under Square's privacy policy. I never see or store your full card number.
  • Spam protection: forms use Cloudflare Turnstile to check that a real person is submitting them. It does not use advertising cookies.

Why I collect it

  • To answer your questions and schedule your training.
  • To plan safe, effective sessions for your dog, including knowing about bite history, health issues and vaccinations.
  • To keep a record of the waiver you signed and the sessions you purchased.

Where it is kept

  • Form submissions are emailed to Threshold K9's business email account and, for intake forms, recorded in a private business Google Sheet.
  • Booking and payment records are kept in Square.
  • This website is hosted on Cloudflare, and form emails are delivered through Resend.

What I will never do

  • I do not sell, rent or trade your information.
  • I do not share it with anyone except the service providers listed above, a veterinarian or emergency contact if your dog's or someone's safety requires it, or when the law requires it.

How long I keep it

I keep client records for as long as they are needed to provide training and to meet business, tax and legal obligations. Signed waivers are kept as long as they may be legally relevant.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information I hold about you by emailing {{email}}. Some records, like signed waivers and payment records, may need to be kept for legal reasons, and I will tell you if that applies.

Age

Training services are for adults. Anyone signing the waiver must be 18 or older.

Changes
