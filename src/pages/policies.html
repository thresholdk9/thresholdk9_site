Client policies

The ground rules

Clear expectations protect your time, my time, and the progress your dog is making.

Getting started

  • Every new client begins with the Threshold Evaluation ({{evalPrice}}, {{evalMinutes}} minutes, in your home).
  • Before the first session, new clients complete the intake form and sign the liability waiver online. The waiver covers all future sessions and packages for that dog.
  • A current rabies certificate is required before the first session.
  • Returning clients can purchase new session packages without another Evaluation.

Booking

  • Sessions can be booked online up to 6 weeks ahead.
  • Training happens in your home or out in the real world, within about a 30-minute drive of Longmont ({{serviceArea}}).
  • Text and video support is available {{supportHours}}.

Cancellations and no-shows

  • Please give at least 48 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule.
  • Cancellations with less than 48 hours' notice, and missed sessions, are forfeited.

Late starts

  • There is a 15-minute grace period for late starts.
  • Sessions still end at their scheduled time, so a late start shortens the session.

Packages

  • Session packages (6 or 12 sessions) expire 12 months after purchase. Unused sessions do not carry over after that date.
  • Package sessions are booked through the client booking link you receive after purchase.

Refunds

  • All Evaluations, training packages and memberships are final sale and non-refundable.
  • This includes Evaluation + bundle purchases, even if the Evaluation shows a different program is the right fit.

The Ascension Track

  • Enrollment is by invitation only, following a Threshold Evaluation.
  • Membership is {{ascensionPrice}} per month with a {{ascensionTermMonths}}-month initial commitment, under a separate membership agreement.

Questions
